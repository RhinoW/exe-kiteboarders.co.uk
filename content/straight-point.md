---
title: "Straight Point Firing Range"
---

Throughout the year, the Ministry of Defence operate a live firing range from Straight Point (at Sandy Bay / Devon Cliffs).

Public safety in the vicinity of the danger area out to sea is a priority and is managed by the Ministry of Defence by using radar, cameras, lookout sentries and a dedicated Range Safety Boat, ‘Smit Cerne’. 

<strong style="font-size:150%; color:red">It is essential that you do not venture into the danger area when the range is in use.</strong>

If you plan to transit this area when the range is in use be aware that the danger area extends a long way out to sea.

## Usual firing times

These times can vary at short notice - check the times below, call on <a href="tel:01395 272972">01395 272972</a> or VHF Ch 11 "Straight Point Range" or "Smit Cerne" and look out for the red flags.

### Summer

* Weekdays: 9am to 4pm (may be extended)
* Evenings: from 30 mins before dusk until 11.45pm
* Weekends: 9am to 12.30pm

### Winter
* Weekdays and weekends: 9am to 4pm (may be extended)
* Evenings: from 30 mins before dusk until 9:30pm

[GOV.UK - Straight Point Range firing times](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/straightpoint-and-wcta-grenade-range-firing-times)
